Research Use Only: What Does It Mean?

“Research use only” is a designation that separates laboratory research materials from products marketed for human or veterinary application. For a research-focused website, that distinction should be clear in product information, advertising, and purchasing context.

What research use means here

True Sciences currently states that its products are intended strictly for in-vitro laboratory research and educational use and are not for human or veterinary consumption, ingestion, or administration.

Why the distinction matters

Research materials can be discussed in terms of analytical methods, documentation, laboratory applications, specifications, and testing. Those topics are different from making claims about treating, preventing, diagnosing, or improving a human or animal health condition.

What a research-focused page should contain

  • Clear research-use language
  • Accurate analytical and documentation information
  • Batch-specific COA references when available
  • No human dosing instructions
  • No treatment or disease claims
  • Clear disclosure when affiliate compensation may be received

Research documentation versus medical claims

A COA can document analytical results. Supplier testing information can describe laboratory analysis. Neither should be presented as evidence that a research material is approved, safe, effective, or appropriate for human or veterinary use.
